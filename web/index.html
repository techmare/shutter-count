About

Reads the shutter count stored in Nikon, Sony and Canon EOS R3, R5, R6, R6 Mark II, R7, R8, R10, R50, R100, RP, 250D, M50, M50 Mark II, M6 Mark II and PowerShot G5 X Mark II photos. The photo is read on this phone and is never uploaded. Added to the Home Screen it works offline, and Safari keeps your history. Reading a camera over a USB cable needs the Android or Mac app: iPhone browsers can’t talk to cameras.
